YIMA — PRIVACY POLICY

Effective date: 27 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Yima mobile app ("Yima", "the app", "we", "us") handles information when you use it on Android or iOS. Yima is provided by Yima, Cambodia.

In short: Yima calculates your Bazi chart and daily fortune entirely on your phone. We do not run a server for the app, we do not ask you to create an account, and the birth details you enter never leave your device. The app shows ads from Google AdMob; Google may collect some device information to show and measure those ads, as described below.

1. Information You Enter (Stays on your Device)

When you use the app you may enter:

  • first and last name (optional);
  • gender, date of birth and time of birth;
  • birth country, birth place and living country;
  • a profile photo (optional), taken with the camera or chosen from your photo library.

This information is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage, and is used only to calculate and show your Bazi chart, luck pillars and daily fortune. We do not receive, upload, sell or share it.

The app also stores your settings on your device: language, light/dark appearance, text size, your saved charts (History) and which chart is "My chart".

2. Permissions

  • Camera and photo library: only if you choose to set a profile photo. The photo is cropped, compressed and saved on your device. It is never uploaded.
  • App Tracking Transparency (iOS): the app may ask whether advertisers may use your device's advertising identifier. Ads still appear if you decline; they are then less personalized.
  • Notifications: only if you turn on the daily fortune reminder. The messages are prepared on your device and nothing is sent to us. You can turn it off in Profile or in your device settings.

The app does not use your location, contacts or microphone.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Yima is free and is supported by ads from Google AdMob (native ads and full-screen ads). To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google and its partners may collect and process information from your device, such as:

  • the device advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA, where allowed);
  • IP address, approximate location derived from it, device model, operating system and language;
  • information about the ads shown and your interactions with them.

This collection is done by Google under Google's own policies, not by us. We do not receive personal information about you from Google; we only see overall, anonymous statistics in our AdMob account.

Your choices:

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form before ads are personalized. You can change your choice at any time in Profile → "Ad privacy choices".
  • On iOS, you can allow or refuse tracking when asked, and change it later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Google → Ads, or Privacy → Ads, depending on the device).

More information:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. App Updates and Store Services

  • To tell you when an update is required, the app asks Google Play (Android) or the Apple App Store (iOS) which version of Yima is available. This request is made by your device to Google or Apple and does not include your birth details or name.
  • The app needs an internet connection to show your readings. To know whether you are online, it briefly opens a connection to google.com. Nothing is sent over it: no birth details, name or other information.
  • If you choose to rate the app, the review prompt is shown by Google Play or the App Store.
  • Links you open from the app (for example this policy, our website, email or Telegram) open in your browser or the related app, which have their own privacy policies.

5. Crash Reports and Usage Statistics (Google Firebase)

To fix problems and improve the app, it uses Google Firebase:

  • Firebase Crashlytics: when the app crashes or has an error, it sends a crash report (the technical error, device model, operating system and app version).
  • Firebase Analytics: anonymous usage statistics, such as which screens are opened, when a chart is created or a card is shared, the app version, device model, language and approximate country.
  • Firebase Remote Config: the app downloads settings from us (for example how often ads may show).

These never include your name, birth details or photo. Google processes this information under its own policies: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

6. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade any information.
  • We do not create user accounts or profiles.

7. Keeping and Deleting your Information

Your information stays on your device until you delete it:

  • delete a single chart in History (swipe it to the left);
  • remove your profile photo in Profile;
  • delete everything by uninstalling the app, or by clearing the app's data in your device settings.

Because we never receive your birth details or photo, we cannot see, restore or delete them for you.

8. Security

Your data is kept in the app's private storage on your device, protected by your device's own security (for example your screen lock). Please keep your device secure. The texts built into the app are stored encrypted.

9. Children

Yima is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the app are provided by Google AdMob under Google's policies.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy, for example when the app or the law changes. The new version will be published at the same address with a new effective date. If the changes are important, we will also tell you in the app or in the store listing.
